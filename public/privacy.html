Privacy

Microphone and transcripts

The microphone stays off until you tap Start. Ordinary audio is processed live and is not recorded or stored. An explicitly enabled debug session may retain diagnostic transcripts for up to 48 hours.

Website analytics

We use Umami, a self-hosted, cookieless analytics tool on servers in Germany; it processes the visitor’s IP address and browser details to derive a rotating session hash for counting visits, without storing raw IP addresses.
